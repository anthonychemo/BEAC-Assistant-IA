ANNOUNCEMENT
ISSUANCE OF TREASURY BILLS
On the Il th of august 2021, the Public Treasury of the Republic of Cameroon shall, by way of
auction, issue Fungible Treasury Bills with the following characteristics:
• Title 26-weeks Treasury Bills
• Issuance Code CMI200001061 BTA-26 II-FEVR-2022
• Maturity date 020d of febuary 2022
• Re-imbursement In fine
• Nature of securities Dematerialized securities
• Issuance volume (in million CFAF) 25000
• Unit face value (in CFAF) 1000000
• Return Interest shall be pre-computed from the face value of the
bonds
• Subscription deadline Wednesday, Il th august 2021 before 9 a.m.
Banks and financial establishments approved as primary dealers
by the Minister incharge of Finance of the Republic of Cameroon
• Publication of results Wednesday, Il th august 2021 before 12noon .
• Settlement date Friday, 13th august 2021 before 3p.m.
• Value date Friday, 13th august 2021.
