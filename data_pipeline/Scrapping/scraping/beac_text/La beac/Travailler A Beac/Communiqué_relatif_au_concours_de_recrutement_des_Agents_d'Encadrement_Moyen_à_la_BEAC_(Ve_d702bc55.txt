ANNOUNCEMENT
Applicants for the recruitment of mid-supervisory staff
at BEAC are informed that the list of eligible
candidates for written tests is being displayed at the
respective national head offices of each of the 6
member states (Bangui, Brazzaville, Libreville,
Ndjamena, Malabo, Yaoundé) and equally at the
BEAC headquarters in Yaoundé.
Candidates will receive their convocation by email
between March 30th and April 5th. The written exam is
scheduled on April 16th and 17th, 2016.
No information on the modalities and content of the
tests will be given.
After consulting the lists and after April 10th, any
request for further information may be made by email
to beac.recrutement-2016@apave.com
No Information will be given by phone.
